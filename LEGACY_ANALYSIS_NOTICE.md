# Legacy Analysis Notice

## Status
The repository contains linguistic diagnosis documents produced before the current **grammar-discovery-first** methodology was established.

The following are therefore classified as **legacy/preliminary analysis**, not primary evidence:

- `LDRSTLK000MASTERGRAMMAR08172026FDA.docx`
- every numbered-corpus file whose name begins with `LDRSTLK`
- everything under `Linguistic_Diagnosis_Reports/`

## Discovery-phase rule
These files must not be used to decide what structures exist in the corpus. They may contain conventional Kurmancî grammatical categories or conclusions derived from prior knowledge, and using them during discovery would contaminate the experiment.

Do not delete them. Preserve them as a historical record and for a later blind comparison.

## When they may be reopened
Reopen legacy analyses only after:
1. corpus text has been independently recovered and audited;
2. recurrent structures have been recorded with neutral labels;
3. candidate rules have survived cross-corpus testing;
4. a provisional corpus-derived grammar exists.

At that later stage, compare the independently derived system against the legacy analyses and external linguistic literature. Agreement, disagreement, and missing phenomena can then be studied without changing the evidence trail that produced the corpus-derived grammar.

## Primary evidence hierarchy during discovery
1. source documents/images;
2. faithfully recovered corpus text with provenance;
3. direct corpus observations;
4. corpus-derived hypotheses and counterexamples;
5. cross-corpus validation.

Legacy diagnoses and external grammars remain outside this hierarchy until the comparison phase.
