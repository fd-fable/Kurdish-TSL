## Evidential rule

The comparison uses the mechanically complete Deep Dictionary V1 packages as active quantitative evidence. Exact spellings may be linked across sources, but the system does not automatically equate them as lemmas or meanings.

The project preserves earlier LDR/DSR studies. However, agent-generated Stage-4 grammatical studies remain outside the active proof chain where the independent audit marked them failed, circular, contaminated, or provenance-incomplete.

## Non-destructive convergence

The combined layer does **not** replace `001_...` through `014_...`. It points back to them. Every combined observation must remain recoverable to named source-specific evidence.
