## Two comparison layers

The project deliberately preserves two different convergence views:

- **Documentary exact-form layer** — every mechanically preserved exact form remains represented, including digits, metadata/front matter, and other documentary material.
- **Audited lexical-candidate layer** — letter-bearing/no-numeric forms are compared separately and repeated identical first-context reuse is flagged as a possible document/template effect. Language membership remains unresolved.

## Evidential rule

The comparison uses the mechanically complete Deep Dictionary V1 packages as active quantitative evidence. Exact spellings may be linked across sources, but the system does not automatically equate them as lemmas, meanings, pronunciations, grammatical categories, or historical identities.

The project preserves earlier LDR/DSR studies. Agent-generated Stage-4 grammatical studies remain outside the active proof chain where the independent audit marked them failed, circular, contaminated, or provenance-incomplete.

## Non-destructive convergence

The combined layer does **not** replace `001_...` through `014_...`. It points back to them. Every combined observation must remain recoverable to named source-specific evidence.
