## 2. LAYER B: INTERPRETIVE HYPOTHESES

* **Supporting Layer A Evidence**: 28 occurrences in Unit 1 (`namê te`, `neqşê xame`, `nameya îşq`).
* **Competing Hypothesis H001b**: `S001/S002` are post-syntactic phonological clitics attaching blindly to the right edge of noun phrases.
* **Decisive Evidence Needed**: Spoken acoustic stress data. `[CROSS-CORPUS TEST RESERVED FOR LATER COMPARATIVE PHASE]`.

### 2.2 Hypothesis H002: Positional Tendency of Class D04
* **Proposed Structural Interpretation**: Within Study Unit 1, members currently grouped as Class `D04` occur at the defined hemistich-final position in 74 of 88 relevant units (84.09% empirical fit). One possible explanation is a broader syntactic ordering preference.
* **Competing Explanations**: Poetic structure, end-rhyme constraints, metrical cadence, authorial stylistic choices, hemistich segmentation methods, or complex interactions among them.
* **Counterexamples**: All 12 non-conforming instances itemized in Register EXA (e.g. EXA-001 `di kî bi bal xweve celb`, EXA-004 `Mewcûd-i kirin, xerez çi adem`).
